Services

Four practice areas.
One unified lens.

Our services aren't sold as packages. Every engagement is custom-scoped to the decision in front of you—drawing on whichever capabilities deliver the answer fastest, with the most rigor.

01 Practice Area

Primary Research &
Expert Engagement

Direct dialogue with the people who shape commercial outcomes—KOLs, payers, prescribers, advocacy leaders, and patients. We design custom research engagements that surface the signals most market reports miss.

  • In-depth interviews with KOLs, payers, and prescribers
  • Patient journey research and unmet need assessment
  • Advisory board design, facilitation, and synthesis
  • Quantitative surveys with statistical rigor
02 Practice Area

Competitive
Intelligence

Anticipate competitor moves before they reach the market. We track pipelines, decode positioning strategies, and translate signals into the playbook your commercial team needs to defend or disrupt.

  • Pipeline tracking and indication landscape mapping
  • Conference & congress monitoring with strategic synthesis
  • War-gaming and competitor scenario planning
  • Standing intelligence programs with regular pulse updates
03 Practice Area

Analytics &
Forecasting

Forecasts that hold up under scrutiny. We build patient-based models, scenario frameworks, and uptake curves grounded in real-world dynamics—not optimistic assumptions or generic benchmarks.

  • Patient-based forecasting and epidemiology modeling
  • Scenario analysis and Monte Carlo simulations
  • Real-world data analytics and claims-based studies
  • Pricing & access modeling, including payer mix analysis
04 Practice Area

Strategic
Consulting

When the question is bigger than research alone. We integrate insights across functions to inform launch readiness, indication prioritization, lifecycle strategy, and portfolio decisions.

  • Launch strategy & commercial readiness assessments
  • Indication sequencing and lifecycle planning
  • Portfolio strategy and asset prioritization
  • Business development support: target evaluation & due diligence
